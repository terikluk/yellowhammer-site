There was a time when business was simpler. Not easier — simpler.

You knew the person you were working with. You shook their hand. Their word meant something, and so did yours. If something went wrong, they made it right — not because a contract forced them to, but because that's just what you did for people. And when the work was finished, you didn't lose touch. You had a relationship.

Somewhere along the way, a lot of that got lost. These days, "support" often means a chatbot, a ticket number, and a company that forgets your name the moment the invoice clears.

We started Yellowhammer Studios because we believe the old way is still the best way.

## Who we are

We're a mother-daughter team based right here in Huntsville, Alabama. That's not a marketing angle — it's just who we are. When you work with Yellowhammer, you're not routed through account managers or handed off to strangers. You're working with the same two people from the first conversation to launch day and long after.

We build websites, brands, and creative content for businesses that have a story worth telling. And in our experience, that's every business — because behind every business is a person who took a risk, worked hard, and built something.

## What we believe

**Good work shouldn't be out of reach.** We've seen too many small business owners priced out of a professional website or brand, stuck choosing between something they can't afford and something that doesn't represent them well. Our goal is to offer solutions that are genuinely affordable — real craftsmanship at a price a small business can actually say yes to.

**Reliability is a form of respect.** Answering when you call. Doing what we said we'd do, when we said we'd do it. Being there after launch, not just before the check clears. None of that is flashy, but it's rare — and we think it's what care actually looks like.

**Relationships over transactions.** We don't want to build your website and disappear. We want to be the people you call when something breaks, when you have a new idea, when your business grows into its next season. Some of the work we're proudest of isn't a design at all — it's the trust our clients place in us year after year.

## Why we do it this way

For us, this isn't just a business philosophy. It's a matter of faith.

We believe God has blessed us — with each other, with the ability to do work we love, and with clients who have become friends. And we believe blessings aren't meant to be stockpiled; they're meant to be passed on. The way we know how to do that is through our work: treating people fairly, charging honestly, showing up reliably, and caring about our clients as people first.

Scripture says to do everything as unto the Lord, and that's the standard we hold our work to — not perfection, but faithfulness. Every website we build, every brand we design, every late-night fix and quick phone call is a chance to serve someone the way we'd want to be served.

## Our handshake to you

So here's our promise, the old-fashioned kind:

We'll be honest with you, even when it's not what you want to hear. We'll charge you fairly. We'll do the work with craft and intention. We'll answer when you reach out. And we'll still be here — for the updates, the questions, the next big idea — long after launch day.

If that sounds like the kind of partnership you've been looking for, we'd love to meet you. Reach out and tell us your story. The handshake's waiting.

*Yellowhammer Studios is a mother-daughter creative studio in Huntsville, AL, specializing in website design, branding, and social media — made with craft and intention. Always.*
