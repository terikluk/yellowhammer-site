Meet Your Doctor

Dr. James Sailors, DC

With over 15 years of experience, Dr. Sailors brings a patient-centered approach to every appointment. A graduate of Logan University College of Chiropractic, he has spent his career helping patients in the Huntsville community move better, feel better, and live better.

Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing chronic pain, or simply committed to your long-term health, Dr. Sailors will take the time to understand your goals and build a plan that fits your life — not a template.
