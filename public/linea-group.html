Residential Design

Commercial Architecture

Architecture does not stop at the wall. Our interior planning practice integrates spatial logic, materiality, and light to create interiors that are as considered as the structures that hold them.

The Studio

Marco Linea

Founding Principal

With over two decades of practice across three continents, Marco brings an uncompromising eye for proportion, material, and light to every commission. A graduate of the Architectural Association in London, he founded Linea Group with the conviction that architecture should outlast its moment.

Elena Voss

Design Director

Elena leads the studio's interior planning and residential practice, translating architectural intention into the lived experience of a space. Trained at ETH Zürich and the Pratt Institute, she has earned a reputation for interiors that feel inevitable — as though they could not have been any other way.
